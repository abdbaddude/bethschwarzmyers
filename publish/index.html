            Why this monitor exists

            Nigeria’s National Single Window (NNSW) TRS Intelligence & Trade Assurance platform turns periodic WCO-style Time Release Studies into a living decision system — so border delays are measured, attributed, and acted on, not only reported.

            Aligned with WCO TRS Guide v4 (2025), WTO TFA Art. 7.6, AfCFTA trade-facilitation aims, and the NNSW / NRS digital architecture.

            North Star

            24-hour median release for routine import consignments — with staged near-term and medium-term trajectories visible in Executive KPIs and Trends.

            Six capabilities this platform delivers

            End-to-end measurement

            Port-to-gate timelines with median and p95, not single-point averages.

            Delay attribution

            Stage, agency, and process bottlenecks with share of total dwell.

            Agency scorecards

            Peer benchmarks (e.g. Lomé, Tema) and accountability views.

            AI anomaly detection

            Flags unusual dwell so operators look critically, not only retrospectively.

            Corrective-action path

            Workstreams A–D in-app; Benefits tab tracks owners, deadlines, expected vs measured dwell, and assurance status.

            Independent assurance

            Trade assurance posture complementary to NNSW operational systems — not a substitute for them.

            Strategic context (summary)

            • Periodic TRS exercises are necessary but insufficient for continuous improvement.
            • This monitor diagnoses where time is spent, who owns the dwell, and whether performance is moving toward the 24h North Star.
            • It is designed to sit within Nigeria’s NES / NNSW digital architecture as an intelligence and assurance layer.
            • Economic stakes: lower trade costs, more predictable release, and credible facilitation evidence for partners and investors.
