# Strategic Context — NNSW TRS Intelligence

- **B** Attribute delays (stage / agency) → Network / Agencies
- **C** Detect anomalies & quality gaps → Anomalies / Observatory
- **D** Recommendations & benefits tracking → Benefits tab

## Economic stakes
Lower trade costs, more predictable release, credible facilitation evidence for partners and investors.
