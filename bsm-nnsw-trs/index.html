Assurance Statement (Prototype)

Based on the illustrative dataset of 1,240 import consignments at Tin Can Island Port (Q3 2027), the median end-to-end release time is 120 hours. Agency permits and port gate-out operations account for 55% of total dwell time. Continuous monitoring and anomaly rules surface structural delays that a single periodic TRS would understate. Independent re-measurement after priority interventions is recommended to provide formal assurance of improvement.

Source: Nigeria NSW TRS Dashboard (Illustrative) | WCO TRS methodology (Version 4, 2025) | Not actual government data.
